Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Checkpoint is a game library and playtime tracker. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. In this policy, "Checkpoint", "we" and "us" mean the developer of the Checkpoint app.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier. We request only the basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile). If you sign up with an email and password instead, we store your email address and a password hash handled by our authentication provider. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier, the email address Apple gives us (which may be a private relay address) and, the first time only and if you choose to share it, your name.
  • Your profile. Your display name, username, bio and avatar. Your avatar is the picture you upload, or the one from your Google account.
  • Your library. The games you add and, for each one, your status, rating, a review if you write one, hours played, purchase price, favourite flag, the platform you played on and when you marked it completed, plus any play sessions and notes you log. You can edit and delete your play sessions.
  • Your lists. The lists of games you make, with their titles, descriptions and whether each one is public.
  • Steam information (optional). If you connect Steam, you sign in on Steam's own page ("Sign in through Steam"), and Steam tells us the Steam ID of the account you signed in to; we store that Steam ID. We never see your Steam password, and Steam tells us nothing else about your account. A Steam account can be connected to one Checkpoint profile at a time: connecting it removes it from any other profile it was on. We use it to fetch your owned games and playtime, how many achievements you have unlocked in each game you played, and a game's full list of achievements when you or another signed-in member opens that game's achievements on your profile (another member only while you let them see your achievements). Other signed-in members can see your Steam ID (it is the link on your profile's Steam icon). After you have imported your library once, we also refresh it automatically in the background about twice a day while you are actively using the app, so your playtime stays current. When Steam reports more playtime for a game than at the previous refresh, we log the difference as a play session, dated when Steam says you last played it. This works only if your Steam profile's game details are public.
  • Connected services (optional). Which of PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Discord, Instagram and Twitch you have marked as connected, and the PSN id, Xbox gamertag and the Discord, Instagram and Twitch usernames you enter for them (Steam is covered above). Other signed-in members can see them: they are what the icons above the bio on your profile open.
  • Social activity. Which members you follow.
  • Reports and blocks. The reports you send about a profile, a photo, a list, a review or a discussion post (what you reported, why, and when), and the members you block. We also keep a record when we suspend an account (why, and until when). Reports are read by us only: the member you reported is not told who reported them.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry (sentry.io), the error reporting service we use. A report holds the error and where in the app's code it happened, the app version, the operating system version, the device model and the language. It does not hold your account, your name, your email address, your IP address, your library, anything you typed, screenshots, or which screens you visited, and addresses, ids and tokens that appear in an error message are removed before it leaves your device. Reports are kept for a limited time and then deleted. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy > Send crash reports.
  • Search terms. When you search for a game, the search term is sent to our server so it can look the game up.
  • On your device. The app keeps your sign-in session, your recent searches, a saved copy of your library and profile so it works offline, a running play timer (which game and when it started) and which wishlist releases it has already told you about. Files you export (your library and play sessions as CSV) are saved where you choose and never sent to us; a file you import is read on your device, and only the game titles in it are sent to our server to look the games up.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, show your library and statistics, import your Steam games, let you find and follow other members, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and the app does not include advertising or analytics tools; the only thing it sends besides what is described here is a crash report when it crashes (see above). We also use it to keep Checkpoint safe: to filter, review and act on content that breaks the Terms of Use (see Moderation below).

Who can see what

  • Private to you: your email address, the purchase price you enter for a game, and the notes you write on play sessions.
  • Visible to other signed-in members: your display name, username, avatar, bio and the month you joined; your library (each game with its status, your rating and review, hours played, the platform you played on, when you completed it and whether it is a favourite) and so the totals made from it; your public lists (private lists are visible only to you); what you post in the discussions (your threads and replies, with your name and photo); your latest play sessions (the game, hours, platform and when, without notes); who follows whom; and the accounts you connected: your Steam ID, PSN id, Xbox gamertag and Discord, Instagram and Twitch usernames. Your Steam achievement progress (how many you unlocked in each game) is shown on your profile, and a game's achievements are shown when a member opens that game there, unless you turn off Show Steam Achievements in Settings > Privacy; then other members see none of your achievements.
  • Blocking. The members you block are known to you only. They no longer see you follow them, and you no longer see their profile, library, reviews, public lists or discussion posts.
  • Nothing in this list is visible to people who are not signed in.
  • Your avatar image is stored in a public file bucket, so anyone with its link can view it.

Moderation

Members' words are public to other members, so we keep them within the Terms of Use. Text you save in your profile, reviews, lists and discussion posts is checked automatically for slurs and child-abuse phrases and is not saved if it contains them. You can report any profile, photo, list, review or discussion post, and block any member. We read reports, remove content that breaks the rules and suspend the accounts behind it. To do that we can see the reported content, the report and the reporter's account; this information is used for nothing else. Deleting your account deletes the reports you made, your blocks and any record of a suspension of your account.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication, file storage and server functions.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google".
  • Apple provides "Sign in with Apple".
  • IGDB (a Twitch service) provides game information. Our server sends it your search terms; it does not receive your identity.
  • Gaming news sites (for example IGN, Eurogamer and GameSpot) publish the public feeds the News screen shows. Our server reads those feeds; it sends them nothing about you. Opening an article takes you to the site itself, under its own privacy policy.
  • Sentry receives the crash reports described above.
  • Steam (Valve) provides "Sign in through Steam", which you use on Steam's own page to connect your account; Steam then confirms to our server which account you signed in to. The Steam Web API receives your Steam ID when you import your library and when it is refreshed in the background.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy. We share data with them only as needed to provide the features above.

Google user data

Checkpoint uses your Google account only to sign you in and to fill in your name, email address and profile picture. We do not read or store any other Google data, and we do not share Google user data with third parties except the service providers listed above that run the app. Checkpoint's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Settings > Account > Delete account, or by emailing us at the address below. Deleting it removes your profile, library, reviews, discussion posts, play sessions, lists, follows, blocks, the reports you made, uploaded avatar and sign-in. Crash reports do not contain your account, so they are not part of it. Games you added to the shared game catalog remain available to other members, but are no longer linked to you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing the address below. You can edit your profile, remove games from your library and delete your account inside the app at any time. You can also remove Checkpoint's access to your Google account in your Google Account settings under "Sign in with Google".

Children

Checkpoint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
