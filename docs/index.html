Gamebit

A game library and playtime tracker. Keep track of what you play, how long you play it, and what you think of it.

What you can do

  • Build your library. Search for games and add them with a status: playing, completed, on hold, dropped or planned.
  • Log your hours. Record play sessions with a platform and a note, and see your totals and your week at a glance.
  • Rate and favourite. Give games a star rating and pin up to four favourites to your profile.
  • Import from Steam. Connect your Steam ID to bring in your owned games and playtime, and load achievements per game.
  • Follow other players. Find people by name or username, follow them and see what they play.
  • Make lists and join the discussions. Collect games in lists, and talk about them on the boards.

Signing in

Gamebit needs an account: searching for games, building a library and finding other players all happen after you sign in, with your Google account, Sign in with Apple (on iOS) or an email and password. With Google, Gamebit only asks for your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) to create your account. It does not access any other Google data.

Your data

Gamebit is social, so what you add to it is visible to the other members who are signed in. That is your profile (display name, username, photo and bio), your library with its ratings, reviews and hours, your public lists, what you post in the discussions, your latest play sessions, who follows whom, and the accounts you connect (your Steam ID, and your PSN, Xbox, Discord, Instagram and Twitch names). You can hide your Steam achievements in Settings > Privacy. Your email address, the prices you enter and the notes on your play sessions stay private, and nothing is visible to people who are not signed in.

You decide who you see: you can block any member (their profile, library, reviews, lists and posts disappear for you) and report profiles, photos, lists, reviews and discussion posts that break the Terms of Use. Read the full Privacy Policy to see exactly what is stored and how to delete it.
