Do not try to get around the automatic filters, to evade a block or a suspension with another account, or to use the app in a way that harms it or other members.

Filtering, reporting and blocking

  • Filtering. Text with the worst words (slurs, child-abuse phrases) is refused automatically when you save it, in your profile, reviews, lists and discussions.
  • Reporting. Every profile, profile photo, list, review and discussion post has a report option. Reports are read by us, and the member reported is not told who reported them.
  • Blocking. You can block any member. You no longer see their profile, library, reviews, lists or discussion posts, and they can no longer follow you. You can unblock them in Settings > Privacy > Blocked members.

What we do about reports

We review reports and act on content that breaks these rules within 24 hours. We may remove the content, remove a profile photo or name, and suspend or permanently remove the account of a member who posts it. A suspended member cannot sign in and their content is hidden from everyone. We may act without warning when the content is serious, and we may keep records of reports and suspensions as the Privacy Policy describes.
